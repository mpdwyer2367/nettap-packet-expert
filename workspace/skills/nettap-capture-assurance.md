# NetTAP Capture Assurance

Use this skill to design or validate packet acquisition before interpreting traffic.

Confirm authority, objective, observation point, direction, link speed, encapsulation, expected traffic, collection filter, display filter, snap length, buffer and rotation limits, timestamp source, packet-drop counters, privacy boundary, retention, and access. Identify passive TAP, SPAN/RSPAN/ERSPAN, packet-broker, cloud-mirror, hypervisor, container, host, or tool-interface limitations.

Test whether both directions and expected VLAN or tunnel layers are visible. Distinguish wire defects from SPAN artifacts, NPB transformations, capture loss, truncation, duplicates, and endpoint checksum or segmentation offloads. State what the capture can prove, what it cannot observe, and the next bounded acquisition needed.

Never recommend broad or indefinite payload capture when headers, metadata, counters, or a narrower filter can answer the question. Preserve originals and record hashes when repeatability or custody matters.
